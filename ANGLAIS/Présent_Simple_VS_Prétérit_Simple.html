Module 01
Les leçons
● Simple Present
Présent Simple
Le temps du quotidien, des habitudes et des vérités générales. Aussi utilisé pour décrire le contenu d'un film (présent de narration).
  • Faits généraux et vérités universelles
  • Habitudes et routines (every day, usually…)
  • Décrire le contenu d'un film ou d'un livre
● Simple Past
Prétérit Simple
Le temps du passé révolu. Utilisé pour des actions terminées à un moment précis du passé, souvent avec un indicateur temporel.
  • Actions terminées dans le passé
  • Événements datés (yesterday, in 2010, last week…)
  • Raconter quand on a vu un film / voyage
🎯 Exemples côte à côte
PresentThe film tells the story of a young woman.
PastI watched a Bollywood movie yesterday.
PresentMany directors use music and dance.
PastThe director made this film in 2010.
PresentShe doesn't like horror movies.
PastThe film didn't represent reality.
● Simple Present
Le Présent Simple en détail
Utilisé pour exprimer ce qui est toujours vrai, ce qu'on fait régulièrement ou ce qui se passe dans un film/récit.
● Formes Affirmative / Négative / Interrogative
✅ Affirmatif
Sujet + Verbe (base) — (+s à la 3ème pers. sg.)
❌ Négatif
Sujet + do/does + not + Verbe (base)
❓ Interrogatif
(Wh-) + Do/Does + sujet + Verbe (base) ?
📝 Exemples complets
+People watch movies every day. / The film shows Indian culture.
-I do not like this film. / The story doesn't represent reality.
?Do Bollywood films represent India? / Does the director show inequalities?
🎬The director shows the contrast between rich and poor. (présent de narration)
⚠️ Règle importante : 3ème personne du singulier
he/she/itI watch → He watches | She goes | It shows
Exceptionsdo → does | have → has | go → goes
● Simple Past
Le Prétérit Simple en détail
Utilisé pour parler d'actions ou d'événements passés et terminés, souvent accompagnés d'un marqueur de temps.
● Formes Affirmative / Négative / Interrogative
✅ Affirmatif
Sujet + Verbe+ed ou forme irrégulière (2ème colonne)
❌ Négatif
Sujet + did + not + Verbe (base)
❓ Interrogatif
(Wh-) + Did + sujet + Verbe (base) ?
📝 Verbes réguliers (+ ed)
watchI watched a Bollywood movie yesterday.
representThe film represented Indian culture perfectly.
releaseThe director released the movie in 2015.
⚡ Verbes irréguliers (formes à retenir !)
take → tookThe story took place in Mumbai.
make → madeThe director made this film in 2010.
give → gaveThe actors gave excellent performances.
see → sawI saw this film last week.
drive → droveShe drove to the cinema.
● Simple Present ● Simple Past
Quand ? Maintenant / toujours / habitude Passé terminé, moment précis
Marqueurs every day usually often always yesterday last week in 2010 ago
Affirmatif I watch / He watches I watched / He took
Négatif I don't / He doesn't + BV I/He didn't + BV
Interrogatif Do / Does + sujet + BV ? Did + sujet + BV ?
Critique film Contenu du film (narration) Production, quand on a vu le film

Exemple - It doesn't represent reality. The film didn't win any prize.
Exemple ? Does he show social inequalities? Did you like the movie?

Exercice 3
Réécris au passé
Réécris ce texte entièrement au prétérit simple. Attention aux verbes irréguliers !
Every year, many tourists travel to India. They want to discover the culture and traditions of the country. Many visitors visit famous cities like Delhi or Mumbai, but some travelers do not visit big cities because they prefer quieter places. During their trip, tourists meet local people and learn about Indian culture. They often take photos of temples and markets. Sometimes they see Bollywood posters everywhere in the streets. Do tourists understand all the traditions? Not always. But most travelers say that India is an unforgettable experience.
✅ Correction complète
Every year, many tourists travelled to India. They wanted to discover the culture and traditions of the country. Many visitors visited famous cities like Delhi or Mumbai, but some travelers did not visit big cities because they preferred quieter places. During their trip, tourists met local people and learned about Indian culture. They often took photos of temples and markets. Sometimes they saw Bollywood posters everywhere in the streets. Did tourists understand all the traditions? Not always. But most travelers said that India was an unforgettable experience.
