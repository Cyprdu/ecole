📖 Le Cours — India
Capitale & Population

General Information

Capital: New Delhi — Population: 1.4 billion

22 Official languages including: Tamil, Hindi, Gujarati, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, English

Currency: Indian rupee (₹)

Religions

Religions in India

  • 🟠 Hinduism — main religion. Known for being a polytheistic religion, meaning they worship various gods like Vishnu, Ganesh, Shiva, Kali
  • 🔵 Sikhism — strict vegetarian diet, believe in life after death (karma & reincarnation). Easily recognized by wearing a red dot on the forehead (the bindi)
  • ☪️ Islam — significant minority
  • ✝️ Buddhism & Christianity — also present

They also have a strict vegetarian diet, because they consider animals as sacred.

Economy — BRICS

Economy

India is an emerging country and is part of the « BRICS » — a group of 5 countries (Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa) at the beginning, that have very fast growing economies. India's GDP is even higher than France.

Key sectors: Bangalore & Hyderabad are the Indian Silicon Valley — very dynamic IT industry. Half of the American Silicon Valley belongs to Indians (including YouTube).

Tourism is a big part of the economy. The main attraction is the Taj Mahal palace, one of the 7 Wonders of the Modern World.

Géographie

Geography — Neighbours & Cities

Neighbouring countries: Pakistan (NW), China (N), Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Myanmar (ex-Burma)

Disputed territory: Jammu & Kashmir

Major cities:

  • ⭐ New Delhi — Capital (centre-north)
  • 🔵 Mumbai / Bombay — financial capital, Bollywood
  • 🔵 Kolkata / Calcutta — east
  • 🔵 Chennai / Madras — south-east
  • 🔵 Hyderabad — centre
  • 🔵 Bangalore / Bengaluru — south (IT hub)

Rivers: Indus (NW), Ganges (N), Brahmaputra (NE)

Seas: Arabian Sea (W), Bay of Bengal (E), Indian Ocean (S)

India: The Good, The Bad & The Ugly

✅ The Good

  • "Unity in diversity" — very diverse and multicultural country, and yet people feel Indian
  • Successful, vibrant & functioning democracy — largest in the world
  • Emerging economy with a very fast growth rate
  • Influential soft power through Bollywood, cricket and cuisine
  • Bollywood is the only industry to compete with the power of Hollywood

⚠️ The Bad

  • Justice system is very poor and extremely long
  • India has freedom of speech but it is under attack with more and more censorship
  • Poor healthcare and education
  • Deep inequalities between people — extremely poor slums (« Dharavi » in Mumbai)
  • Growing problem of populism

❌ The Ugly

  • Religious discrimination and persecution especially against Muslims and Christians (Narendra Modi's ultra-Hindu nationalist policy)
  • The caste system — rigid social hierarchy
  • Sexual violence against women (rape crisis) — India is deeply patriarchal

Bollywood: True representation of India?

"Bollywood" = Bombay + Hollywood. India's film industry produces more films than Hollywood!

Real India VS Reel India (Real VS Reel India)

Bollywood shows a glamourised version of India — not always realistic:

  • Songs, dance, romance — not always the reality
  • Often avoids showing caste discrimination, poverty in slums
  • BUT: some recent films tackle real social issues (3 Idiots, Slumdog Millionaire)
Famous Politicians

Mahatma Gandhi

He is the national hero of India because he helped the country to gain independence in 1947 from the British Empire — without violence. He was killed by a Hindu extremist.
Famous Politicians

Indira Gandhi

On the left is Indira Gandhi. She was Prime Minister. Next to her is Rajiv Gandhi. She was killed by 2 Sikh extremists, who worked as her bodyguards — as a revenge of Operation Blue Star when she ordered the attack of Sikh armed militants in a temple.
Current PM

Narendra Modi

Current Prime Minister. Known for his ultra-Hindu nationalist policy which has led to religious discrimination against Muslims and Christians. India's democracy is under threat due to rising censorship and populism.
